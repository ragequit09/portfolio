About Me

A little about how I work

I'm a self-taught designer turned front-end developer with a soft spot for clean typography and interfaces that don't get in the user's way. I like projects where craft actually matters — where someone will notice the extra half-day spent on spacing and motion.

Outside of client work, I'm usually rebuilding some part of my own site, reading about type design, or sketching layouts I'll probably never use.

My goal is to keep working with small teams and independent founders who want their product to feel as good as it works — and to keep getting better at the craft with every project.
